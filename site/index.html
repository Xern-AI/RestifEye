FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Does it phone home?

No. No accounts, no telemetry. One optional weekly version check — switchable off.

Will it interrupt my call?

No. Mic, camera, DND or any idle inhibitor defers it. Hard cap: 15 minutes from when the break was due.

Can I escape a strict break?

Hold escape for three seconds. It goes on your own stats, nowhere else.

Won't a long film suppress breaks all day?

No. Video players hold an idle inhibitor, background music doesn't. And timers never reset — the break fires the moment the film ends.

How do I know it's running?

A tray face that changes with your last two hours. Closing the window keeps it running.

Wayland or X11?

Both. Developed Wayland-first on Fedora GNOME, using portal-friendly D-Bus APIs.

Is it really free?

Free, forever. A paid Mac and Windows build may come later.
