BreakTime

Break reminders that respect your flow.
Free and open source, for Linux.

Download for Linux View source

AppImage & RPM · Fedora-first, works on most distributions

📅

Never interrupts a meeting

Microphone or camera in use? Do Not Disturb on? Breaks wait — up to a limit, so waiting can't become skipping.

🚶

Counts breaks you already took

Walked away, locked your screen, suspended? That was your break. No nagging for rest you already had.

⏰

Warns before it acts

A 30-second heads-up before every break. Finish your sentence — the screen is never yanked away cold.

💪

Strict, but fair

A snooze budget per break. When it runs out, the break happens — with an illustrated exercise for your eyes or body.

📊

Local analytics & advice

Screen time, focus stretches, and break compliance — weekly, monthly, yearly — with practical advice from your own patterns.

🔒

Private by architecture

Everything stays on your machine. No accounts, no telemetry. One optional weekly update check — that's the entire network story.

Install

Grab the latest release — the AppImage runs anywhere:

chmod +x BreakTime-*-x86_64.AppImage
./BreakTime-*-x86_64.AppImage

On Fedora, the RPM integrates with your app grid and updates: sudo dnf install ./breaktime-*.rpm
