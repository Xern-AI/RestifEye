Why RestifEye

Strict enough to matter.
Polite enough to keep.

Most break apps get uninstalled the first time they interrupt a call. RestifEye is built around one idea: a reminder you don't hate is the only reminder that works.

📅

Never interrupts a meeting

Microphone or camera in use? Do Not Disturb on? Breaks wait — up to a hard cap, so waiting can't quietly become skipping.

🚶

Credits breaks you already took

Walked away, locked your screen, suspended? That was your break. RestifEye notices and never nags for rest you already had.

🔔

Everything from the notification

A 30-second heads-up before every break with Start now, Snooze, and Skip right in the notification — decide without breaking focus.

💪

Strict, but fair

Snooze and skip budgets you control. When they run out, the break happens — with an illustrated eye or movement exercise, full-screen or windowed, your choice.

📊

Local analytics & advice

Screen time, focus stretches, and break compliance — weekly, monthly, yearly — with practical advice computed from your own patterns, on your own machine.

🔒

Private by architecture

No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry. One optional weekly update check against GitHub — that is the entire network story, and you can turn it off.

How it works

Out of your way, until it shouldn't be

1

It starts with your computer

RestifEye launches at login and sits quietly in your tray. Eye breaks every 20 minutes, movement breaks every 50 — every interval is yours to tune.

2

A heads-up, not an ambush

30 seconds before each break you get a notification. Start it now, snooze it, or skip it — within the budgets you set for yourself.

3

A real break, with a real exercise

The break screen guides a short eye or stretching exercise with an animated figure. Emergencies happen: a logged 3-second hold always lets you out.

4

Your patterns, your advice

Dashboards show screen time and focus stretches; a rule engine turns four weeks of history into concrete, judgment-free suggestions.

Install

Running in under a minute

Any distribution

AppImage

Download, make executable, run. No root, no dependencies.

chmod +x RestifEye-*-x86_64.AppImage
./RestifEye-*-x86_64.AppImage
Fedora / RPM-based

RPM package

Integrates with your app grid, icon theme, and updates.

sudo dnf install ./RestifEye-*.rpm

Get both from the latest release. On GNOME, install the AppIndicator extension to see the tray icon — KDE, XFCE, Cinnamon and MATE show it out of the box.

FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

Does it phone home?

No. There are no accounts and no telemetry. The only network request RestifEye can make is an optional weekly version check against GitHub Releases, and you can switch it off in Settings.

Can I escape a strict break in an emergency?

Always. Hold the escape button for three seconds and the break ends. It goes on your own stats — accountability to yourself, not to us.

Will it interrupt my presentation or call?

No. While your microphone or camera is in use, or Do Not Disturb is on, due breaks are deferred and rechecked. A hard cap (15 minutes by default) makes sure deferral can't become denial.

How do I know it's running?

A tray icon with a small menu (open, pause, quit). Closing the window keeps RestifEye running in the background; launching it again simply reopens the same instance.

Wayland or X11?

Both. RestifEye is developed Wayland-first on Fedora GNOME and uses desktop-portal-friendly D-Bus APIs — no X11-only hacks that break on modern sessions.

Is it really free?

Yes — free as in GPL-3.0, forever, on Linux. A paid macOS version may fund development later; the Linux app stays free and open.

Using a Mac?

A polished, paid macOS version is on the roadmap. Enough interest is exactly what gets it built.
